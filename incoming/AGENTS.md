# Material inbox

Inherit `../AGENTS.md` and the preservation rules in [raw/AGENTS.md](../raw/AGENTS.md). This directory contains documents awaiting intake or undergoing processing.

Follow the [intake contract](../documentation/specs/add-material.md). Keep source files here until the readable material has been verified; move them to a dated archive only when completing the operation. Do not delete or overwrite originals.

A short intake request requires the agent to execute the full contract and its checks. Do not require extra user instructions about preservation, verification, duplicates, provenance, or unknown data.
