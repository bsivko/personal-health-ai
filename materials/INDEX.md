## How to read this index

The source author's prescriptions and conclusions are separate from the project's conclusions. For imaging and graphical tests, text does not replace images. Incomplete documents and partially legible handwriting are explicitly marked in the cards. Resubmissions and additional scans are linked to the original materials; a new medical event is not created automatically.
