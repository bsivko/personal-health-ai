# Readable materials

Each individual source material is represented here by its complete text and a short card.

Navigation: [INDEX.md](INDEX.md) lists cards; [manifest.json](manifest.json) stores identifiers, checksums, and intake status.
- [undated](undated/AGENTS.md) — accepted reference information with unknown compilation dates.
- Complete text preserves content, tables, units, and reference ranges; a short card is not a substitute.
- Cards state the material identifier, originals, dates, type, summary, and verification status.
- Link text to pages or other available locations in the original.
- Explicitly mark illegible and doubtful fragments. Recognition corrections must be traceable.
- Do not insert your own medical conclusions into document text.
- Consolidate facts across documents in `../records/`; interpretations belong in `../overview/` or `../outputs/`.
- Contract: [adding a material](../documentation/specs/add-material.md).
- Format: [readable material](../documentation/specs/material-format.md). Date directories mirror the originals; an extracted-text block does not replace structured content.
- Content classification: [rules](../documentation/specs/material-classification.md), [code dictionary](classification.json). Record type and domains in both card and manifest; multiple domains are allowed.
