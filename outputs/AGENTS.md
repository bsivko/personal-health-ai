# Operation results

Store appointment packages and analyses as of particular dates.

- Each result states its purpose, preparation date, materials used, and coverage limitations.
- Filenames describe content without `latest`, `current`, "today", or calendar dates. The snapshot directory and an explicit date/time in the contents establish the result's temporal scope.
- Evidence links must lead back to the document and original.
- A result remains a historical snapshot. New information requires a new version explicitly linked to the previous one.
- Distinguish facts, hypotheses, recommendations, and questions for a specialist.
- Contracts: [appointment preparation](../documentation/specs/prepare-visit.md), [health analysis](../documentation/specs/analyze-state.md).
