# Ongoing measurements

Inherit `../AGENTS.md`. Ongoing series follow observation dates, not import batches. Preserve provenance, units, date precision, and corrections; do not merge measurements solely because they share a day.

Contract and provisional format: [measurements](../../documentation/specs/measurements.md). Historical sources remain in materials; create a [derived database](../DATABASE.md) for joint queries if needed. Do not edit observation JSONL and the derived database as independent copies of the same fact.

Create individual measurement directories as corresponding observations arrive.
