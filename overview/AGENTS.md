# Medical overview

Store a compact health overview and timeline based on materials and facts.

- State the update date, source coverage, and known gaps.
- Link statements to `../records/` or directly to `../materials/`.
- Explicitly distinguish observations, clinicians' statements, and assumptions.
- Update the overview when data changes; it does not replace primary sources.
- Keep historical appointment packages and analyses in `../outputs/`.
- Until data is available, do not create a medical overview or fill one with assumptions.
