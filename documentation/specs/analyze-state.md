# Health information analysis

Status: provisional contract. Requirement: R-004 in [REQUIREMENTS.md](../REQUIREMENTS.md).

## Input
Question or analysis purpose, period, and available materials. Without a specific purpose, a general overview with explicit coverage is acceptable.

## Result
An analysis in outputs with date, question, source list, and limitations:
- Observations: values outside source reference ranges, changes over time, conflicts, and missing information.
- Hypotheses with evidence, uncertainty, and data that would help clarify them.
- Recommendations and questions for a specialist; support external medical claims with current authoritative sources.

## Verification
- Check important source values against materials and their verification status.
- Consider units, ranges, dates, and available context; do not combine dissimilar measurements without justified comparability.
- Do not equate an out-of-range value with a diagnosis or a hypothesis with an established fact.
- Check current external sources for medical recommendations and cite them in the result.
- Ensure each observation is traceable to data and unknown information is not presented as a normal result.

Do not write analytical conclusions into records as confirmed facts. When transferring to overview, preserve interpretation status and evidence links. New material may require reconsidering a result.

## Practical contract verification
Use real materials to check reproducibility of source observations and transparency of the transition from facts to hypotheses. Medical validity requires separate assessment; the existence of a document does not establish it.
